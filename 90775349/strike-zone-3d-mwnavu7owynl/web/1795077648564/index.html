Taktik 3D otishma: har raundda dushmanlar ko’payadi. Hammasini yo’q qiling va tirik qoling.

Kompyuter: WASD – yurish, sichqoncha – qarash, chap tugma – otish, R – zaryadlash, Space – sakrash, 1/2/3 – qurol (3 – snayper), o’ng tugma – nishonga olish (bosib turing).
Telefon: chap tomon – joystik, o’ng tomon – qarash, o’ng pastdagi tugmalar – otish, sakrash, zaryad, qurol, NISHON (bosib turing – nishonga oladi, qo’yib yuborsangiz o’chadi).
